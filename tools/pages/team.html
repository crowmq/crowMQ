How to get involved

The most useful contributions at this stage are not code.

  • Review the protocol. Ambiguities found before the specification freezes are cheap; afterwards they are permanent.
  • Share a workload. A description of real agentic traffic — message mixes, dependency patterns, arrival behaviour — makes the benchmark environment representative rather than plausible.
  • Tell us where adaptation would not help. Identifying the cases where deterministic handling wins is an explicit project goal.
